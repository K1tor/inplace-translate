InPlace Translate is a simple, powerful text translation tool.

The story begins in a small village where a young developer worked late into
the night. She wanted to share her favorite novel with friends around the
world, but every translation tool she tried would only show the result on
screen — never writing it back into the file itself.

So she built her own tool. It reads a file, translates every paragraph, and
saves the result right back where the original text lived. Subtitles keep
their timelines. Lyrics keep their timestamps. Markdown keeps its code
blocks. And JSON files keep their keys and structure, with only the values
translated.

Today the tool supports Microsoft Bing, Google, DeepL, and any OpenAI
compatible service. One command, and the whole world can read your words.
